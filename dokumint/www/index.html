Frequently Asked Questions

What is dokumint.ai?

dokumint.ai is a comprehensive document processing and storage solution. You can upload a wide variety of documents in a number of formats and the platform analyzes each document with the help of AI and extracts relevant information for insight analysis or summary creation. The platform can also store original images of documents for longer term retrieval purposes

Is dokumint.ai free?

dokumint.ai uses a credit based pricing mechanism. One credit is consumed for every document page processed. All users receive 100 credits free every year and can purchase additional credits for as low as $0.08*

How can dokumint.ai help me?

dokumint.ai aims to digitize the document clutter in our professional and personal lives. On average, an individual processes 5 documents a day in the form of mailed statements, purchase receipts and email documents; that's over 1750 documents in a year. Accessing, processing and analyzing these documents over the long term becomes challenging and cumbersome. dokumint.ai can help by creating a simple, unified store for all document information with easy ability to sort, store and search.

Is dokumint.ai secure?

dokumint.ai uses industry standard encryption during the transmission and processing of documents. No personal information is collected or stored and all user information is partitioned to avoid security breaches.

What types of documents can be processed?

The platform supports multiple document types and formats. Current capabilities include the ability to process expense receipts, invoices, purchase orders, utility bills, medical reports, property tax statements, education reports among many others. We are continuously adding functionality to support more document types so watch this space for more information in the future.

What if i Have more questions?

You can find answers to additional questions on our Help portal or by contacting us at support@dokumint.ai
